Privacy Policy

Vote To Survive Studio ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the Vote To Survive channels. Last updated: November 17, 2026.

Who uses the app

Only the channel owner. The app is not offered to the public and does not let anyone else sign in.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (through YouTube API Services): upload videos to the owner's own channel, read the channel's videos, public comments and statistics, and read YouTube Analytics reports for the channel.
  • Instagram and TikTok: publish the owner's own videos and read their comments and statistics, when connected.

What the app stores

Video files, statistics and the text of public comments are stored only on the owner's own computer, to count votes and show results. Comment authors are stored as pseudonymous identifiers. Authorization tokens are kept in the operating system's credential store and are never shared.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising.
  • It does not access any account other than the owner's.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the channel's features you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at Google security settings.

Deleting data

To ask for removal of any comment data the app holds, write to votesurvive@gmail.com. Stored comment data is deleted within 30 days of the request.
